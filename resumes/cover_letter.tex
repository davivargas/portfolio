\textbf{Software Developer Application}

\vspace{10pt}

Dear Hiring Team,

During my internship at ICBC, I learned how differently software must be approached when people depend on it for important services. A small defect could affect an insurance workflow, so writing the feature or test was only part of the job. We also had to understand the requirement, protect the data, investigate edge cases, and communicate clearly across the team. That experience shaped how I build software and is a major reason I am interested in bringing my skills to digital health.

I have carried that mindset into my more recent development work. At Sprinter Delivery, I helped replace a spreadsheet-based operation with a web platform backed by PostgreSQL. I implemented row-level security and database tests that verified one driver could not access another driver's orders, delivery records, or media. At ZipHQ, I built REST APIs and integrations, then tested their behavior against a real PostgreSQL environment so failures could be found before they reached users.

Digital health is especially meaningful to me because reliability, security, and usability have a direct effect on the people receiving and providing care. I would be excited to contribute my experience with TypeScript, Python, FastAPI, REST APIs, automated testing, and cloud-hosted databases while learning the needs of your users and domain. I also value the opportunity to work with developers, clinicians, product partners, and other specialists whose different perspectives lead to safer and more useful software.

I am based in the Vancouver area and am comfortable collaborating both on-site and remotely. I would welcome the opportunity to bring my attention to detail, curiosity, and ownership mindset to your team while growing as a backend developer in a field where careful engineering truly matters. Thank you for considering my application.
